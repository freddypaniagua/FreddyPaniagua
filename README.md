## What I'm building

**[Tipping Point](https://freddypaniagua.github.io/tipping-point-game/)** · TypeScript, Vite<br>
How many blue whales does it take to balance the Eiffel Tower? Every day brings 5 new weigh-ins and one shot at each. Pick your number, then watch the objects pile onto the scale as it swings, wobbles, and settles to show how close you got, followed by a wild fact. Everyone in the world gets the same puzzle each day. [Play today's puzzle →](https://freddypaniagua.github.io/tipping-point-game/)

**CSCAN** · C++17, Qt6, Tesseract OCR<br>
Full buy, force, or save? In Counter-Strike 2 you get about 20 seconds to decide, and a bad call can lose your team the next few rounds. CSCAN watches your screen during the match, reads your money, side, and score straight off the HUD, and tells you what to buy before the timer runs out. It started as a web tool where you typed in your numbers by hand, but that ate half the buy phase, so I rebuilt it to read the game for you. It only looks at the screen and never touches the game itself. *In progress: teaching it to read the HUD at every resolution.*

**Drop Tones** · JavaScript, Web Audio<br>
A DJ battle game where you mix music live and go head-to-head against a bot or a friend. I'm experimenting with two ways to play: "dropping tones" onto little characters that each carry a part of the song, or a DJ mixer like the ones real DJs use to mix tracks and battle each other. Every sound is built to fit together, so anyone can jump in and sound good. *Early development.*
